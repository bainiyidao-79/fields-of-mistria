When Fields of Mistria crashes to desktop or refuses to launch, the cause is almost always one of three things: damaged game files, an outdated graphics driver, or a machine below the game's floor specs. Work through the fixes below in order — most players stop at step one.

## Fix 1: Verify the Game Files

Missing or corrupted files are the most common cause of crashes and boot failures. Steam can repair them automatically:

1. Open your **Steam Library** and right-click **Fields of Mistria**.
2. Choose **Properties**.
3. Open the **Installed Files** tab.
4. Click **Verify integrity of the game files**.
5. Wait for the scan to finish — Steam re-downloads anything damaged or missing, which usually takes under a minute given the game's [tiny install size](/guide/system-requirements).

Launch the game again once the verification completes. If Steam replaced files, this alone fixes most crash loops.

## Fix 2: Update Your Graphics Drivers

An out-of-date GPU driver is the second most frequent culprit, especially right after a big game update like [1.0](/release/release-date):

- **NVIDIA:** grab the latest driver from NVIDIA's site or the NVIDIA App.
- **AMD:** use AMD Software Adrenalin or AMD's driver page.
- **Linux players:** update Mesa (or the vendor driver) through your distribution's package manager.

After installing a new driver, **restart the PC** before launching the game — a reboot flushes the old driver out of memory and prevents half-loaded states from causing the very crashes you are chasing.

## Fix 3: Check Your Hardware Against the Minimums

If the game still crashes, compare your machine against the [official system requirements](/guide/system-requirements). The bar is low — a GTX 460-class GPU and 6 GB of RAM — but machines under it can crash during area transitions or in busy scenes like festivals. Integrated graphics from the last few years clear the minimum comfortably; genuinely ancient hardware may not.

## Still Crashing? Two More Things to Try

- **Reinstall cleanly.** Uninstall, restart, and reinstall from Steam — this rules out any local corruption file verification might have missed.
- **Report it to the developers.** Persistent crashes on spec-compliant hardware are worth reporting through NPC Studio's official channels listed on [fieldsofmistria.com](https://fieldsofmistria.com). Include your OS, GPU, driver version, and when the crash happens. Post-1.0 patches (the first landed August 19, 2026) have been actively fixing stability issues.
