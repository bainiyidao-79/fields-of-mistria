Relationships in Fields of Mistria run on a Heart system: you raise hearts with every villager by talking to them and giving gifts, and reaching **8 Hearts unlocks dating** with a romanceable character. The 1.0 update raised the NPC heart cap to 10 and added Proposal & Wedding events plus marriage and children, so the full romance arc now exists. The game ships **12 marriage candidates** (10 story-introduced bachelors and bachelorettes plus secret ones you unlock through play), and the official FAQ confirms **marriage candidates are not gender-locked** — you can pursue any of them regardless of your character's gender.

## How Hearts Work

- Talk to a character and give them a gift once per day; Kotaku's tips guide notes Mistria's villagers have unusually varied things to say, and talking to them repeatedly raises affection faster than you'd expect.
- Characters have dialogue tiers per heart level; a heart event triggers the day after you cross a threshold. Getting from dating-ineligible to 8 hearts takes real consistency — one update-preview reviewer noted it can take two or more in-game weeks per character if you're spreading gifts around.
- At 1.0, the cap went from 8 to 10 hearts: 9 and 10 Heart events lead to the Proposal and Wedding, and married couples can have children.
- Gifting categories run from Loved → Liked → Neutral → Disliked, with some characters hating specific items. Junk, bugs (with exceptions!), and "weird gifts" backfire on most villagers.
- **Elsie the gossip** is your in-game wiki: after her quest, you can gossip with her once per day to reveal one liked or loved gift for a character (reported by the Jinxed Moth dating-update reviewer).
- Villagers drop hint dialogue in the world too — museum donations, Saturday Market food-cart chatter, and quest hints all telegraph gift preferences.

### Unlocking Seridia

Seridia, the Priestess, is a late-game route: her romance requires reaching **Floor 100 of the Mines** to complete her questline — and to start it you first need **8 Hearts with both Juniper and Eiland**. Her gift list is dragon-themed, with the ironic exception of dragon claws and scales, which she despises.

## Gifting Strategy That Actually Works

1. **Talk daily, gift daily.** Every candidate benefits from consistent contact; heart events fire the day after you level up.
2. **Cook for them.** Several loved gifts are cooked dishes (Apple Honey Curry, Sushi Platter, Summer Salad), so the Cooking skill doubles as a relationship engine — cooked goods also sell high, so nothing is wasted.
3. **Mine for them.** Perfect ores and gemstones (Perfect Copper Ore, Pink Diamond, Perfect Ruby) are loved by March, Adeline, and Balor; golden products (Golden Ingot, Golden Butter, Golden Feather, Golden Cookies) are loved across nearly everyone's list.
4. **Check item tooltips and the Relationships tab** — the in-game UI tracks which liked/loved gifts you've discovered (revealed via Elsie gossip or by giving them).
5. **Never gift the Hated item** and skip junk/bug gifts unless you know a specific character wants them (the kid Luke loves bugs, per an EA reviewer's note).

## Dating Rules After the Romance Update

- Reaching 8 hearts and triggering the confession event starts dating; the next day Elsie visits to explain dates and "date inspiration" items.
- **You can date multiple characters at once.** There's no jealousy dialogue — and no breakup or friend-zone switch either, so choices are sticky until marriage.
- **Two dates per week max**, and not back-to-back days with the same person. Dates end with a photo you can hang up (photos can't be deleted, the reviewer warns).
- Date locations unlock through play: picnic in the Deep Woods and inn dining come early; the bathhouse date requires a certain 100,000-tesserae bar of soap; beach dates come from ocean fishing, park dates from digging on the Eastern Road, and a gem-cutting date from a random Mines treasure chest. The shooting star festival adds a brooch that opens another outing.
- When you do marry (10 hearts, post-1.0), it's one partner only — and with children, your farm becomes a full household.
- Friendship isn't romance: best-friend routes exist with unique dialogue, but (per the same reviewer's testing) they don't include dates or photos. (to be confirmed whether hangouts are added later.)

## Beyond Romance: The Whole Town

With 30+ villagers, relationships extend past the bachelors. Non-romanceable characters drive the story: kids like Luke (bug enthusiast) start side systems, older villagers teach you their trades through quest cutscenes, and the Friday night saloon crowd runs serialized conversations — card games, drinking gossip, and a D&D-style campaign that can play out across a whole season. The wiki sorts the cast into Romanceable, Non-Romanceable, Saturday Market Vendors, and Special Characters — and Hayden's prize hen Henrietta is practically a character in her own right, jealous of any attention Hayden gives you.
