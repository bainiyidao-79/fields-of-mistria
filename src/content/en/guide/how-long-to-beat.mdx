Fields of Mistria has no official playtime, and NPC Studio hasn't published a "main story in X hours" figure. The best evidence comes from reviewer play logs: one creator spent **around 10 hours to reach the end of the first month (28 in-game days)** in Early Access, while a second logged **about 50 hours into a 1.0 playthrough and still called the run "mid game."** A reasonable planning number: expect roughly **40–60 hours to see the town-repair story credits (estimated)** and **100+ hours (estimated)** if you chase 10 hearts, museum completion, and the Mines.

## Documented Playtime Data Points

| Data point | Hours | Scope | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First in-game month | ~10 h | Month 1 (28 days), basic systems learned | YouTube reviewer, Early Access playthrough |
| 1.0 mid-game | ~50 h | New 1.0 save, still mid-game, not finished | YouTube reviewer ("about 50 hours into my 1.0 playthrough") |
| Dating one character | 2+ in-game weeks | 0 → 8 hearts with daily talk + gifts | Dating-update reviewer (time varies by how many characters you pursue) |

Those are individual trajectories, not aggregates — there's no HowLongToBeat-style consensus dataset in our sources yet, so treat every total as an **estimate anchored to those logs**.

## What Makes Up the Hours

The 1.0 release made the game *completable*: the Town Repair Storyline can be finished, Renown now caps at level 100, the heart cap is 10 with weddings and children, and the Mines storyline was completed by The Mines Update. The long tail is everything around that spine:

- **Museum**: 82 sets and 409 items to donate across four wings — a completionist grind on its own
- **Romance**: 12 candidates, each needing sustained daily gifting/conversation to 8 hearts, then 2 more to 10, plus two dates per week
- **Skills**: a long skill list (farming, ranching, fishing, woodcrafting, archaeology, blacksmithing, cooking, plus mining/combat) with perk trees; the level cap was raised to 60 late in development
- **Mines**: floor-by-floor biomes with elevators, a gate every 20 floors, and secrets down to Floor 100+
- **Seasonal life**: festivals, Saturday Markets, and quest boards that keep a "forever farm" loop going after credits

## Playstyle Changes the Number

Both logged reviewers stress the same thing: Fields of Mistria never *forces* the farm. You can play efficiently and burn hours on the town quest board, or lean RPG — one 1.0 reviewer wrote it "feels way more RPG like than a farming sim," since woodworker, blacksmith, or pure-adventurer income paths are all viable. Skipping combat and romance shortens the story; a completionist running the lava caves and the full dating roster will easily triple the mid-game mark. The 50-hour reviewer's own summary is the best caution label: "I personally haven't completed the entire game yet."

## What About Post-Release Time?

Fields of Mistria is live-service in the cozy sense: the studio committed in its roadmap to "post 1.0 updates with additional content and surprises," and a post-1.0 patch landed August 19, 2026. Community expectations of what the next update brings are still forming (to be confirmed — no official post-1.0 roadmap date yet), so an endgame save may keep growing. If you want the full story now, budget a 50–60 hour arc (estimated) and remember the farm doesn't have to end there.
