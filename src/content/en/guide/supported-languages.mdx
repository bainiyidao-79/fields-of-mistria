**Fields of Mistria supports one language: English.** The Steam store page's language section lists English for interface and subtitles — with no other options — and that was true at the Early Access launch in 2024 and remains true after the [1.0 release on August 5, 2026](/release/release-date).

## What "English Only" Covers

Everything text-related runs in English:

- **Interface** — menus, tooltips, the inventory, and settings screens
- **Subtitles and dialogue** — all 30+ villagers' storylines, quest text, and letters
- **Item names and descriptions** — crops, fish, artifacts, gifts, and recipes

There are no official interface or subtitle translations into any other language, so the game's famously heavy writing — one of its best-reviewed features — is only accessible in English for now.

## If You Prefer Another Language

Since the game is a text-rich life sim, language support matters more here than in most indie releases. A few practical notes:

1. **Check the store page before buying.** Steam lists each game's supported languages in a dedicated section; for Fields of Mistria it currently shows English only.
2. **Localization may arrive post-launch.** NPC Studio has promised post-1.0 content updates, and language additions are a common post-launch feature for farm sims of this size — watch the official site or the store page news feed rather than third-party claims.
3. **The store name stays the same.** Even players searching under translated names (the game picked up a large international following during Early Access) end up on the same single English store listing — see our [Steam name guide](/intro/steam-name).

## Does the Language Barrier Affect Gameplay?

Mechanically, no: farming, mining, fishing, and combat are language-independent, and icon-driven menus make daily play navigable without strong English. The trade-off is story and social systems — villager friendships, gift hints, and quest instructions all rely on reading English dialogue comfortably.
