Fields of Mistria costs **$13.99 USD** on Steam (US store), its standard price since Early Access and unchanged at the 1.0 full release. There is a single edition — no deluxe tier, no DLC packs — and every content update, including the 1.0 update itself, has shipped as a free patch on the base game. The lowest recorded price is **$11.19** from a -20% discount.

Steam's store package for Fields of Mistria is a plain $13.99 single-purchase SKU. SteamDB confirms the shop lists just one sellable package (SubID 766534) plus a private beta-testing key package, and the game has **no DLC depots** (`hasdepotsindlc = 0`). In practice that means:

## Value vs. the Genre

For context, Steam's own "More like this" module on the Fields of Mistria page shows: Stardew Valley at **$14.99**, Palia as **Free To Play**, Graveyard Keeper 2 in prerelease at **$24.99** (discounted to $19.99), and Moonlight Peaks at **$34.99** (discounted to $29.74). At $13.99, Fields of Mistria is the cheapest full purchase among these — and it delivers the whole game in one package. Download footprint is tiny too: roughly **305 MB to download and ~615 MB on disk** for the Windows build (SteamDB depot data).

Reviewers consider the price easily earned: one 50-hour mid-playthrough 1.0 review called it "the best and most highest caliber farming sim that we have gotten since Stardew Valley." Whether you're in it for the farming, the 12 romance candidates, or the museum grind, there's no paid content gate.

## Steam Deck and Controller Value

The $13.99 purchase includes full Steam Deck support: the game is **Steam Deck Verified** (tested July 8, 2026 build), with all functionality accessible using the default controller configuration, legible interface text, and good performance on the handheld's default graphics settings. Steam's storefront even promotes it as a "Steam Deck Top Played" title. One caveat from Valve's verification notes: **cross-platform cloud saves are not supported**, so a save started on Deck stays on Deck-class hardware rather than syncing to your desktop (and vice versa).

Buying on Steam also means the standard Steam Subscriber Agreement applies — refund and support policies are Steam's own (see the [store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2142790/) for current terms).
