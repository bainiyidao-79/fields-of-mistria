**Fields of Mistria costs $13.99 USD on Steam** — and that has been the story since day one. The price was set at Early Access launch in 2024 and did not move for the [1.0 release on August 5, 2026](/release/release-date); Steam's listing today shows $13.99 as both the original and current price. Regional pricing exists in other currencies, so the exact figure in your storefront may differ from the US number.

## What the $13.99 Actually Buys

The single purchase includes the complete game: the full [town-repair storyline with its 1.0 ending](/release/release-date), the marriage-and-children romance arc, the Mines, the museum, and every seasonal system. NPC Studio's model has been one price, permanent updates — both the Early Access content updates and the 1.0 upgrade itself arrived as free patches, and the studio's roadmap promises post-1.0 content updates on the same basis.

## Is It Worth It?

For calibration: $13.99 puts it in the same bracket as other premium cozy sims, below most AA indie releases. Reviewers logged [50+ hour playthroughs](/intro/what-type-of-game) before even reaching the endgame, and the game holds an "Overwhelmingly Positive" Steam rating — one of the highest-rated farming sims of its year. If your machine is old, check the [system requirements](/guide/system-requirements): a GTX 460-era GPU clears the bar, which makes the entry price even easier to justify.

## Watching for Sales

Steam seasonal sales are the only discounts the game has seen, with −20% ($11.19) as the recorded low. There is no subscription tier, no bundled edition, and no reason to wait for a "complete edition" — the game you buy today is the complete one.
