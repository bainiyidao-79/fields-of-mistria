**Fields of Mistria 1.0 released on August 5, 2026** on Steam, exactly two years after the game first launched in Early Access on **August 5, 2024**. Developer NPC Studio staged it as a milestone release: the 1.0 update is free for existing owners, finishes the game's core storyline, and adds the marriage-and-children endgame that Early Access players had been waiting on.

## Full Release Timeline

| Milestone | Date | What Happened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Steam demo | June 5, 2024 | A playable demo let players try the farm ahead of launch |
| Early Access launch | August 5, 2024 | The base loop: farming, mining, fishing, romance, and the first Mines chapters |
| Early Access content updates | 2024–2026 | Recurring major updates — including the Mines Update in early 2026, which finished the Mines storyline and raised the skill cap to 60 |
| **1.0 full release** | **August 5, 2026** | Story complete, marriage and children, renown cap raised to 100 |
| Post-1.0 patch | August 19, 2026 | The first maintenance patch after launch |

NPC Studio committed in its roadmap to **post-1.0 updates with additional content**, though no specific post-launch roadmap has been published yet — worth checking the [official site](https://fieldsofmistria.com) for news.

## What the 1.0 Update Actually Added

The headline changes from Early Access, as listed by NPC Studio for the 1.0 release:

- **NPC heart cap raised from 8 to 10**, with Proposal and Wedding events — marriage and children are now in the game
- **Renown (town rank) cap raised to 100**, and the **Town Repair storyline is now fully completable**, giving the game a definitive ending
- Additional dungeon quests and rewards in the Mines
- More museum sets and rewards, plus new Request Board quests
- Stillwell and Zorel join the Saturday Market roster

## Early Access Was Not a Half Game

The two-year Early Access run is the reason the 1.0 launch landed so strong. By the time 1.0 arrived, the game already held an "Overwhelmingly Positive" Steam rating built across those updates — each major patch shipped as a free content drop rather than paid DLC. New players buying today simply get the finished version of that process for [$13.99](/release/price), with no editions or upgrade tiers to sort through.
