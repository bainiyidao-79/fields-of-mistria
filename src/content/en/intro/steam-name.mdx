The exact Steam store name is **Fields of Mistria** — no "The", no subtitle, no apostrophes. That matters more than it sounds: the cozy farm sim niche is crowded with similarly titled games, and the fastest way to the right page is searching the title word-for-word in the Steam client or on the Steam website.

## Fastest Ways to Find the Store Page

1. **Search the full title.** Type `Fields of Mistria` into Steam's search box. The official page is published by **NPC Studio**, which is the quickest tell that you are on the right listing.
2. **Use the direct link.** The store page lives at `store.steampowered.com/app/2142790` — app ID **2142790**, useful for price trackers and review sites.
3. **Check the developer line.** The store page should show NPC Studio as both developer and publisher. NPC Studio is an independent studio, and Fields of Mistria is its debut commercial release.

## Why People Search for the Name in the First Place

The title trips people up in two directions. First, the "of" — search engines and storefront autocomplete frequently suggest "Fields **of** Mistria" misspellings like "Field of Mistria" or "Mistria Fields", and the store search handles most of these gracefully, but exact spelling gets you there in one step. Second, the game picked up a large audience in regions where it is known by a translated name, while the Steam listing itself has always been **English-only** — the store page lists just one supported language, English, even after the 1.0 update. If you were wondering whether a localized version exists under a different listing, it does not: there is exactly one store page, and it is the English one.
