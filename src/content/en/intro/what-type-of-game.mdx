Fields of Mistria is a **farm life simulation RPG** — a pixel-art game where you rebuild a struggling village while running your own homestead. It is developed and published by NPC Studio, sold on Steam for **$13.99**, and it left Early Access with its full **1.0 release on August 5, 2026**. Steam files it under the genres Indie, RPG, and Simulation, and the comparison most players reach for is Stardew Valley — though Fields of Mistria leans harder into RPG storytelling than most games in the category.

## The Short Answer: Cozy Farm Sim With RPG Bones

You inherit an overgrown field on the edge of **Mistria**, an idyllic village tucked between forest and sea. The town was just hit by an earthquake, and the deal is simple: the homestead is yours if you help Mistria recover. From there the loop blends two genres:

- **The farm sim half** — planting crops, raising animals, fishing, foraging, and crafting, all on your own schedule with no fail states breathing down your neck.
- **The RPG half** — descending the Mines to fight monsters, unlocking big one-shot magic spells, following 30+ villager storylines, and pushing the town-repair storyline to its ending.

## What You Actually Do Day to Day

Customization comes first: your character, your house, and the farm layout itself are all adjustable to your taste. After that, the systems stack up:

1. **Farm, fish, mine, and craft** — the four pillars that fund everything else and feed the town's recovery.
2. **Raise animals and breed rare color variants** — breeding is cosmetic but genuinely collectible, with rare seasonal coats to chase.
3. **Romance 12 marriage candidates** — candidates are not gender-locked, and the 1.0 update added proposals, weddings, marriage, and children on top of the dating system.
4. **Unlock magic** — spells are big, memorable effects (refill your energy, water every crop at once) rather than minor stat buffs, and they tie directly into how deep you dare go in the Mines.
5. **Rebuild the town** — quests, museum donations, and shipped goods all raise Mistria's town rank, which unlocks new shops, systems, and story beats.

## How It Compares to Other Farm Sims

Fields of Mistria wears its '90s farm RPG inspiration openly — NPC Studio calls it a spiritual successor to the genre's late-'90s classics. What sets it apart in practice is the writing and the town feeling alive: villagers have jobs, hobbies, and grudges, conversations carry over week to week, and seasonal festivals keep the calendar busy. Combat and magic give it more adventure flavor than the average cozy sim, while quality-of-life touches keep the farming itself low-friction.

It is a **single-player** game, sold only on [Steam for PC](/release/platforms) (Windows and Linux, with Steam Deck Verified support).
