Fields of Mistria is a cozy farming life sim RPG developed and published by NPC Studio, an independent studio. You inherit an overgrown homestead in the seaside village of Mistria and help rebuild the town after an earthquake, while farming, fishing, mining, crafting, cooking, romancing villagers, and uncovering a surge of strange magic. It launched into Early Access on Steam on August 5, 2024, and its full 1.0 release followed on August 5, 2026 at $13.99 USD.

## What You Actually Do in the Game

The pitch is classic: Mistria, an idyllic village tucked between forest and sea, has offered you a full-of-potential homestead in exchange for your help restoring the town to its former glory. Steam's official description calls it "a spiritual successor to the Farm-Sim RPGs of the late 90s & onward" — the Harvest Moon-style loop many players grew up with.

The core features NPC Studio lists on Steam:

- **Customize** your character, home, and farm to suit your style
- **Farm, fish, mine & craft** your way to a revitalized town
- **Romance** — find your ideal match among 12 marriage candidates
- **Raise animals** and breed rare color variants
- **Unlock magic** that aids farming and adventuring
- **Enjoy changing seasons** with new quests, festivals, and even villager outfits
- **Get to know 30+ villagers** and follow their stories
- **Battle foes** and discover treasures in ancient ruins

Your day-to-day loop is tending chores, checking the daily quest board, and pushing your skills forward. Progress is measured through the town rank: quests, museum donations, and selling goods all raise Mistria's renown and unlock new systems as you go.

## Why So People Talk About It

The numbers back the hype. On Steam the game holds an **"Overwhelmingly Positive"** rating — roughly 97% positive across more than 31,000 reviews in all languages (about 24,700 of them English) — and it peaked at **39,233 concurrent players on August 9, 2026**, right after the 1.0 launch. Steam also flags it as one of the **top played games on Steam Deck**.

Press reception is strong too: Polygon called it "pure magic," the New York Times praised its "well-written dialogue," and Kotaku listed it among the most beloved farming sims in recent memory.

## How It Compares to Stardew Valley

Reviewers routinely measure Fields of Mistria against Stardew Valley, and the consensus is that it stands on its own. The pixel art uses thick outlines that recall the Game Boy Advance era, with character portraits styled after '90s anime. The writing gets the loudest praise: nearly every villager has hobbies, flaws, and a reason to exist in the world, with ongoing conversations (like the town's Friday night gatherings) that carry over week to week. Multiple reviewers describe the overall feel as more relaxing and lower-friction than its famous competitor, with quality-of-life automation baked into the design.

## Where to Play It

Fields of Mistria is sold exclusively on Steam for PC at $13.99 (Windows and Linux, with Steam Deck Verified status) as of the 1.0 release. Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, and Xbox versions have **not** been released; NPC Studio's official FAQ says the team is "currently evaluating additional platform support." If you want it now, the Steam [store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2142790/) is the only official storefront.
